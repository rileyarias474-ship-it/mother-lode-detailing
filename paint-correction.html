What it fixes

Swirls out. Gloss back.

Paint correction is machine polishing that levels the top of the paint. It takes out swirls (the fine, circular scratches that show up in direct sun), light scratches, and paint that has gone dull or oxidized.

Scratches that go all the way through the clear coat need paint, not polish. Send photos and we'll tell you which kind you have.

FAQ

Good questions.

When will paint correction be available?

It's coming soon. Ask through the booking form or by phone for the latest.

How much will it cost?

Starting at $400 for the full body. Condition matters, so photos help us give you a real number.

Can it fix every scratch?

No. It takes out swirls, light scratches, and dull, oxidized paint. Scratches through the clear coat need paint.

Should I do it before a ceramic coating?

Usually, yes. A coating locks in the finish under it, so correcting the paint first gets you the best result.
