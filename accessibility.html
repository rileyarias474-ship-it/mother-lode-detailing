Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Last updated September 14, 2026

Mother Lode Detailing wants everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, or other assistive technology. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

What we've done

  • Text descriptions for photos and videos
  • Labels on every form field and clear error messages
  • Full keyboard access, a "Skip to content" link, and visible focus outlines
  • Color contrast checked against WCAG AA
  • Pause buttons on videos, and less motion for visitors who turn on reduced motion in their device settings
  • The dust-wiping effect at the top of the page is decorative. The "Wipe it for me" button does the same thing without dragging.

Known limitations

  • The work videos have no sound or spoken content. Each one has a short text description.
  • Social media sites we link to are run by other companies, and we can't control their accessibility.

Need help or found a problem?

If anything on this site is hard to use, tell us and we'll help you get what you need, including booking by phone. Call or text (559) 505-5164 or email arias.riley@yahoo.com. We aim to reply within 2 business days.
