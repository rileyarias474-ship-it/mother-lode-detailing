FAQ

Good questions.

Do you charge more for towns farther out?

No. Every town on this page gets the same starting prices, with no extra charge for the drive.

Do you come to me, or do I drop off?

We can come to you, or you can drop the vehicle off. Tell us which works better when you book, and we'll sort out the details when we confirm your day.

My town isn't listed. Can you still help?

Out on a back road, or just over the line in Amador or Tuolumne? Ask anyway.

How do I get a price?

Text or email a few photos with the year, make, and model, or use the booking form. We reply with a price and the next open days.
