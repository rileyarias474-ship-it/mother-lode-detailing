Detailing for Lodi.

Lodi is our one stop outside Calaveras County, out in San Joaquin County wine country, with Highway 12 running straight from town up into the foothills. Vineyard dust, summer heat that bakes bugs onto the paint, and hard water spots from sprinklers are all fair game.

Book an exterior detail, an interior detail, or a ceramic coating so the next wash is easier.

We can come to your home or work in Lodi, or you can drop the vehicle off. Either way, it's the same price, with no extra charge for the drive.
