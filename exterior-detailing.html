Recent work

Real trucks, real dirt.

Around here, paint takes a beating from red clay on the rockers, sap and pollen from the pines and oaks, bugs on every grille that runs Highway 4, and hard water spots from sprinklers. An exterior detail is built for exactly that.

FAQ

Good questions.

How much is an exterior detail?

It starts at $80 for sedans and $100 for trucks and SUVs. The final price depends on condition, so send a few photos for an exact quote before any work starts.

Can you get pine sap and bugs off?

Yes. Pine sap, bug splatter, oak pollen, brake dust, and red clay are exactly what this service is for.

Do you come to me?

We can come to you, or you can drop the vehicle off. Tell us which works better when you book, and we'll sort out the details when we confirm your day.

Can I add the interior?

Yes. A full inside-and-out detail covers the exterior and interior in one visit, starting at $200.

How long does it take?

That depends on the vehicle and the service. We'll give you a time estimate when we confirm your day.
