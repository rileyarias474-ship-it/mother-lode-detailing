What it does

Gloss now, easier washes later.

A ceramic coating is a liquid layer that hardens on top of your paint. It adds gloss, and dirt and water slide off instead of sticking, so red clay, pollen, and road film rinse off easier and every wash goes faster.

It's not armor. Rock chips and deep scratches can still happen, so a coating is about keeping the paint looking good and easy to clean, not about making it bulletproof.

Price

FAQ

Good questions.

How much is a ceramic coating?

$750 to $1,200, depending on the vehicle and the paint's condition. Send a few photos for an exact price before any work starts.

Is a coating worth it on a work truck?

If the truck lives on dirt roads, a coating makes every wash quicker, because dirt and water slide off instead of sticking.

Does a coating stop rock chips?

No. A coating adds gloss and makes washing easier, but rock chips and deep scratches can still happen.

Do you come to me?

We can come to you, or you can drop the vehicle off. We'll talk through what works best for a coating when you book.

How long does it take?
