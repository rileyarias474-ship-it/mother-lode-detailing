Privacy policy

Effective September 15, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Mother Lode Detailing ("we," "us"), a California business, collects through this website and when you contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels, or tracking tools.
  • The booking form sends what you fill in, and any photos you attach, straight to our email. Nothing from it is stored on this website.
  • We use your information only to respond to you and do the work you ask for.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.

Information we collect

Information you choose to send us

When you text, call, or email us, including by using the booking form, we receive what you include, such as:

  • Your name and phone number, and your email address if you email us
  • Your vehicle's year, make, and model, and the service you want
  • Your town, the days that work for you, and any notes
  • Photos of your vehicle, if you attach them

When you press "Send my request," your browser sends the form to our website, which turns it into an email to us. The website keeps no copy. Resend, Inc. delivers that email for us and handles it under its own privacy policy. If you use the text or email button instead, your browser hands a pre-filled message to the app on your device, and we only receive it if you press send in that app; your mobile carrier or email provider then handles it under its own privacy policy.

Photos you attach are resized in your browser before they are sent, which strips camera information such as GPS location out of the file.

Information collected automatically

  • Website hosting logs. Like most websites, our hosting provider, Vercel Inc., automatically records technical details when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you request, and the date and time. These logs are used to run and secure the website. See Vercel's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts. This website loads its fonts from Google. To deliver them, your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address and browser information. Google Fonts does not set cookies. See Google's privacy policy.

We do not use cookies, local storage, analytics tools, advertising networks, social media pixels, or session recording on this website.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your request, give you a quote, and schedule your service
  • To perform the detailing work and follow up about it
  • To keep basic business records, such as past jobs and payments
  • To comply with the law and protect our rights

We will not send you marketing texts or emails unless you ask to receive them. If you ask us to stop contacting you, we will.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We only disclose it:

  • To service providers that make our work possible, such as our phone carrier, email provider, website host, and the service that delivers our booking form email
  • If required by law, or to protect our rights, property, or safety
  • As part of a sale or transfer of the business, in which case this policy continues to apply

Photos of your vehicle

We will not post photos of your vehicle online without your permission. When we do post work photos, we hide license plates.

How long we keep information

We keep messages and job records for as long as we need them to serve you and keep business records, generally no longer than 3 years after your last service or message, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we have about you
  • Correct information that is wrong
  • Delete your information
  • Stop texting or emailing you

To make a request, email arias.riley@yahoo.com with the subject "Privacy request," or call or text (559) 505-5164. Tell us the name and phone number or email you used with us so we can find your records and confirm the request is from you. We will respond within 45 days. We will not treat you differently for making a request.
